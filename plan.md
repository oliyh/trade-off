# Remaining interview topics

Resume here — answer any of these (any order) and they'll get folded into
`interview-notes.md`, then eventually drafted into the blog post per `CLAUDE.md`.

## 1. Ask forgiveness vs declare upfront
A specific example of each approach to driving change, and what made you choose one
over the other that time.

## 2. AI adoption counterexample
What did you actually observe — timeline, who mandated it, what changed. (Notes.txt
speculates it was mandated top-down out of fear of being left behind, and happened
fast — worth confirming/correcting and getting specifics.)

## 3. Your own first day / onboarding story
A real, specific memory to ground the "new skater on the ice" metaphor — either your
own first day at a Big Org, or onboarding someone else.

## 4. Personal qualities — concrete moments
- One "stupid question that mattered" — a time asking something basic (what does
  that acronym mean / why do we do X before Y / is this on the critical path)
  changed the outcome.
- One "left it better than I found it" moment — doesn't require 30 years, just a
  concrete instance.

## 5. Closing / audience
- Who's in the room — conference name/audience type (affects how much
  banking-specific detail vs general Big Org framing to keep).
- The one thing you want them to walk away with.
